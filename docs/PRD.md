# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# Neural AI Website

**Version:** 3.0  
**Status:** Draft  
**Project Type:** Frontend UI/UX Design & Responsive Website

---

# 1. Project Overview

Neural AI is the official Artificial Intelligence Society of Delhi Technological University (DTU). The society serves as a hub for students passionate about Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, Robotics, and emerging technologies.

The objective of this project is to design and develop the official digital identity of Neural AI—a modern, responsive website that showcases the society's community, legacy, projects, events, research, learning resources, and achievements.

The website should reflect an active student community that learns, builds, collaborates, competes, and innovates together.

Rather than resembling a commercial AI startup, the experience should feel like the official website of a prestigious technical society with modern aesthetics and exceptional usability.

# 8. Design Vision

The website should communicate:

- Innovation
- Community
- Technical Excellence
- Learning
- Collaboration
- Research
- Leadership
- Student Culture

The design should feel like the official digital home of an elite university technical society.

The website should be content-rich, engaging, and approachable while maintaining a premium visual identity.

---

# 9. Visual & Brand Direction

The uploaded Neural AI logo serves as the foundation of the visual identity.

The interface should adopt the logo's cyan-to-purple gradient as an accent while keeping the overall interface clean and restrained.

## Design Principles

- Modern
- Professional
- Academic
- Community-driven
- Minimal yet content-rich
- Elegant
- Spacious
- Accessible

The visual hierarchy should emphasize real content over decorative elements.

## Visual Style

- Clean layouts
- Consistent spacing
- Subtle glassmorphism
- Rounded corners
- Soft shadows
- High-quality illustrations
- Real event photography
- Project showcases
- Timeline visuals
- Modern iconography

## Avoid

- Generic AI SaaS layouts
- Oversized marketing hero sections
- Excessive empty space
- Heavy neon effects
- Cyberpunk aesthetics
- Overly corporate styling
- Visual clutter
- Generic college website templates
